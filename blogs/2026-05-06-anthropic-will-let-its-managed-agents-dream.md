In April, Anthropic launched the public beta of Managed Agents, its platform for running AI agents on its infrastructure. On Wednesday, the company announced it is expanding Managed Agents with the ability to dream, focus on outcomes, and orchestrate multiple agents. These new features, the company says, will “make agents more capable at handling complex tasks with minimal steering.” What do AI agents dream about? The marquee feature here is “dreaming,” which is now in research preview.
