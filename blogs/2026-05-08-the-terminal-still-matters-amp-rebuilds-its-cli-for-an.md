AI coding companies clearly agree that autonomous agents are becoming central to software development, but how those systems should operate — and where developers fit into the loop — is still up for debate. Amp, the AI coding startup that spun out of Sourcegraph in late 2025, revealed this week a rebuilt version of its CLI, dubbed Neo, redesigned to be remote-controllable, plugin-powered, and better suited to longer-running agent workflows. At the same time, the company has also been arguing that “the coding agent is dead.
